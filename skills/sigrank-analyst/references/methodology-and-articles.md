# Teach the leaderboard from evidence

The Active / Total / All-time board ranks the returned public operator population by published Yield. Explain **what is observed first**: aggregate fresh input (I), generated output (O), cache writing (W), and cache reading (R) for the returned scope. Then show the operating ratio `R/I : 1 : O/I` as the memorable shape of the cascade. For example, `19:1:0.09` reads as about 19 cached-context tokens reused and 0.09 output tokens for each fresh-input token. That is a composition of token flow, not a rating of the person or their work. The three-part ratio omits W; Construction (`W/R`) describes context creation separately. Yield (`R×O/I²`) combines the ratio's reuse and generation terms; two operators can have similar Yield with very different operating ratios.

When a user asks for **raw telemetry analysis**, distinguish three levels:

1. **Public board/profile aggregate:** the MCP supplies period-level I/O/W/R counts and published metrics where present. Analyze observed totals, complete four-pillar shares, leverage, velocity, operating ratio, construction, denominator sensitivity, and provenance. Keep source window, population and units visible. These aggregate pillars are the closest telemetry this public plugin can inspect.
2. **User-supplied aggregate:** on an explicit request, use the [manual preview guide](manual-preview.md). Mark counts and every result unverified; do not silently assign a rank or measurement class.
3. **Private source records:** local per-session/per-turn Tokenpull or provider logs are outside this remote plugin. It cannot inspect prompts, messages, cumulative usage events, device history or model-level traces. Do not promise anomaly or temporal analysis that needs those records. This Claude package asks for a public codename and does not inspect local identity files or token logs.

If the user asks for a specific article or a current quote, open that page and cite it. Describe the article's date/cohort and separate its thesis from the live operator facts. Some marketing pages use words such as “skill,” “productivity,” or “useful output” more strongly than the methodology supports. Preserve the method's narrower claim: SigRank observes AI operating structure; it has not established work quality, intelligence, or economic value.

The Four Degrees of Leverage on the [Four Degrees of Leverage wiki page](https://signalaf.com/wiki/four-degrees) (the stable reference; the live site design may also show the chart on the homepage) is a useful teaching ladder, not a substitute for an Active cohort statistic. It labels a static AA modeled reference, an Operator Center of Mass median, a median of the top 100 power users, and a top evaluation, with Yield, SNR, Velocity, Leverage, 10xDEV and cache : input : output operating ratios. Explain **who each column represents and why a median is used for a skewed field** before comparing values. The site can show a broader research/seeded population and a different update date than the isolated plugin's claimed-operator Total board. For a focal Active operator, use matching `get_field_stats(window="all", view="total")` as the direct distribution; only then add the Four Degrees ladder as explicitly separate context. Do not quote the ladder's “top 100” as an Active-field median if the MCP population is below 100.

Quote those rows only after checking that the live page actually exposes them. Mean counts describe scale and composition; ratios of mean counts are not the existing median metrics. The modeled baseline has no observed absolute token counts. The methodology release aligns its Key Figures to the analyst's claimed, non-retired, ranked Active / Total / All-time selection and identifies the refresh time; until that release is live, treat an unlabeled methodology median as separately scoped rather than substituting it for matching tool statistics.
